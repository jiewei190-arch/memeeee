# SHOGA v2 — Ritual Club

An alternate, membership-first version of the SHOGA site: a status sidebar
(current batch, live countdown to the next club shipment, club open) beside
one column of labelled sections. On phones the sidebar becomes a top bar with
a swipeable status strip, the join card opens as a bottom sheet, and a join
bar stays at the bottom of the screen.

Static, no build step: `python3 -m http.server` in this folder.

Prices, discount and the monthly ship day for the countdown are in `CONFIG`
at the top of `main.js`.
